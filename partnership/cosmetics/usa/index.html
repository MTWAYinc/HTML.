The U.S. Cosmetics Reality

Fast trends.
Real margin.

The U.S. indie cosmetics market is growing explosively, with TikTok and Instagram driving trends fast enough that a campaign built for last quarter's aesthetic can already feel dated.

Margins in this category typically run 70 to 80%, which gives real room for performance-based media spend, but only for brands that can keep up with how quickly the content and creator landscape moves.

Inclusivity and representation aren't optional positioning choices here, they're baseline expectations that shape how a campaign gets received.

I · Trend Velocity
TikTok and Instagram move fast enough that campaigns built around last quarter's aesthetic already feel dated, creative refresh has to be constant, not occasional.
II · Creator Partnership Dependency
Influencer and creator partnerships are close to essential in this category, brands without an active creator strategy are competing with one hand behind their back.
III · Inclusivity As Baseline
Representation and inclusivity aren't differentiators anymore, they're baseline expectations, and campaigns that miss this get called out publicly and fast.
